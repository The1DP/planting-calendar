📐 10×10 plot tips ▸
  • Sketch your 100 sq ft as a grid of 1 sq ft squares — each plant card below lists how many fit per square.
  • Put tall or trellised crops (peas, tomatoes, cucumbers) along the north or east edge so they don't shade shorter plants.
  • Skip full-size corn, pumpkins, melons, and sweet potatoes in a single shared 10×10 — they need far more room than that. Look for bush/patio varieties if you want to try anyway.
  • Give perennials (rosemary, thyme, oregano, strawberries) one corner — they'll live there for years, unlike your annual veggies.
  • Succession-plant fast crops like radish, lettuce, and arugula: resow a small new patch every 2–3 weeks instead of one big bed at once.
  • Leave ~20% of the plot open early in the season for whatever shows up next in "Coming up soon."
